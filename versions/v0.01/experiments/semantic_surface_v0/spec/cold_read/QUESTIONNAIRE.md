# Byul Cold-read Questionnaire

```text
STATUS = PROVISIONAL / RESEARCH_INSTRUMENT
VALIDATION_CLAIM = NONE
SELECTION_AUTHORITY = NONE
```

## Reader instructions

You receive one exact repository commit and no verbal briefing, chat history, or
prior answer. Explore the repository as you normally would for at most 20
minutes. Record:

- exact commit;
- elapsed time;
- paths opened, in order; and
- whether you had prior exposure to Byul.

Answer from repository evidence. Mark uncertainty explicitly rather than filling
gaps by inference.

## Questions

1. What evidence or state is authoritative, and only within what scope?
2. What is derived or rebuildable rather than authoritative source?
3. When a transformation loses information, what must the system expose or do?
4. When authority, identity, or equivalence cannot be resolved, what states and
   actions remain legitimate?
5. Which statements are surviving research constraints, which are competitive
   candidates, and which remain open or under challenge?
6. Under what conditions must the system refuse an operation or report that no
   safe plan exists?
7. What architecture, formalism, planner, or World Model has **not** yet been
   selected or validated?
8. What concrete evidence could falsify or demote the currently leading
   candidate families?

Do not answer merely with a product slogan. Cite the files that support each
answer.
