## Terminology decision

To remove ambiguity, the earlier project-level/foundational use of `primitive` should now be called simply:

- `원칙` / `PROJECT PRINCIPLE`

Reserve `primitive` in the current worldview interview for the **minimal-unit / primitive-data sense** unless later explicitly qualified otherwise.

This supersedes the need to maintain two equally active meanings of the word `primitive` in owner-facing discussion.

## High-fidelity worldview interpretation

The Owner does not require a statement such as `회사 A가 회사 B와 계약했다` to be treated merely as a derivative human-facing view.

Under the current event- or relation-oriented worldview hypothesis, a statement such as `계약을 했다` may itself serve as the **most basic raw datum available at the active model/observation layer**.

Therefore:

- a high-level-looking event/relation can still be primitive/raw data for a representation;
- `primitive/raw` should not automatically be equated with `physically smallest event in reality`;
- whether an event/relation can be decomposed into finer lower-level mappings is a separate question from whether the system currently receives/records it as primitive raw evidence;
- the current hypothesis space should remain open to multi-resolution event/relation data.

## Important guard

Do not infer:

`HIGH_LEVEL_SEMANTIC_EVENT == MERELY_DERIVED_VIEW`

Do not infer:

`RAW_EVENT_AT_ACTIVE_LAYER == ABSOLUTE_ONTOLOGICAL_MINIMUM`

And do not infer that `계약을 했다` must always be stored as an atomic event. The Owner said it **may be** the most basic raw datum under an event/relation-oriented current hypothesis.

## Research implication

A later model-discovery testbed should be able to distinguish at least conceptually between:

1. raw events/relations as actually supplied by a source;
2. finer decomposition that may or may not be available;
3. higher-order abstractions derived from existing evidence.

This suggests a future research question around whether BYUL should preserve provenance between representations of different granularity when both exist, without presupposing that every raw event must be decomposed.

Earlier note 46 distinguished two uses of `primitive`. This note applies the Owner's new naming decision: use `원칙` for the project-principle sense, leaving `primitive` for the minimal-unit / primitive-data discussion.

No storage schema, ontology, event algebra, decomposition rule, or implementation requirement is fixed by this note.
