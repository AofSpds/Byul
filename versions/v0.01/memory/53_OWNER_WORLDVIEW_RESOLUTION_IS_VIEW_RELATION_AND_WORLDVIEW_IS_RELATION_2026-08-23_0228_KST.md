## Owner statement

> "네 전혀아닙니다. 당연히 해상도도 view라는 관계에 의해 결정이 될수 잃습니다

> 월드뷰라는 것도 일종의 관계라고 봅니다"

## High-fidelity interpretation

- The Owner explicitly rejects the idea that a world model should have one globally uniform active resolution.
- Resolution itself may be **conditioned by the relation/view** through which the underlying relation/event bundles are being represented.
- Therefore, different regions, relations, or purposes in the same world model may legitimately coexist at different effective resolutions.
- The Owner also treats a `worldview` not as an external privileged frame but as **a kind of relation** in the current worldview hypothesis.
- This suggests that `view`, `resolution selection`, and `worldview` may belong to the same broad relational/compositional universe rather than being separate meta-layers imposed from outside.

Conceptually:

```text
relation/event bundle B
  + relation/view R1
      -> resolution r1 / View V1
  + relation/view R2
      -> resolution r2 / View V2

WORLDVIEW W
  may itself be represented as a relation participating in the same broader structure.
```

## Important guards

Do not infer:

`WORLDVIEW_IS_RELATION -> FIXED_RELATION_SCHEMA`

or

`VIEW_DETERMINES_RESOLUTION -> ONE DETERMINISTIC ROUTING FUNCTION`

or

`MULTI_RESOLUTION -> EVERY REGION MUST ALWAYS USE DIFFERENT RESOLUTION`.

The exact formalism, source/target roles, recursion rules, and implementation representation remain OPEN.

## Relation to prior Owner statements

This is consistent with earlier direct Owner statements that:

- multiple valid views can exist over the same relation bundle;
- view formation is relation-conditioned;
- apparent atomicity versus bundle structure depends on abstraction/resolution;
- routing/chain/twin are implementation candidates only, not the worldview itself.

## Interview implication

Avoid further asking whether global uniform resolution is required. A more useful unresolved axis is what the Owner means by `요구성능` when selecting a view/resolution: semantic fidelity, computational cost, reconstruction ability, task utility, or some relation-specific combination.

No architecture, routing rule, resolution function, worldview registry, or implementation requirement is fixed by this note.
