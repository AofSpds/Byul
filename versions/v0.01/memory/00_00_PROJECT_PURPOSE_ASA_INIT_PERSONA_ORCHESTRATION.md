## 1. Why this project exists

The immediate purpose of this BYUL / ASA-MI research is to discover and test the modeling/orchestration requirements needed for **ASSET AI AGENT ASA INIT**.

This is fundamentally a **Persona Orchestration research project**.

The Owner's central questions are not merely how to implement a fixed number of agents/personas, but more basic:

- Is a Persona one, two, or three?
- Is such counting/classification even necessary at all?
- How can apparently different Personas emerge or be represented?
- How can two become three?
- What does split / merge / mutation / succession mean under different Views?
- How should relations, memories, roles, purposes, and abstractions compose so that Persona structure can evolve rather than be frozen in advance?

## 3. High-fidelity interpretation

### 3.1 Persona orchestration is the practical target

The worldview, relation-network, abstraction/View, composition, identity, succession, merge/split/mutation, and model-discovery discussions are not philosophical exploration for its own sake.

They are being pursued because ASSET AI AGENT ASA INIT needs a Persona orchestration model that does **not prematurely freeze** assumptions such as:

- fixed Persona count;
- fixed Persona boundaries;
- fixed one-persona-one-object identity;
- fixed split/merge categories;
- fixed relationship semantics;
- fixed abstraction resolution;
- fixed mapping from human identity to Persona identity.

### 3.2 Human as bundle is an implementation/worldview hypothesis, not proven ontology

A major design intuition is that a human need not be modeled as one indivisible, permanently unitary consciousness. A bundle/process/relation-oriented framing may be more useful for Persona orchestration.

This does **not** establish a scientific claim that humans are literally a bundle in a final ontological sense.

The point is to avoid making a rigid one-human-one-static-persona assumption that would block later evolution.

### 3.3 Shell / membrane / wrapping metaphor

The Owner has used metaphors such as:

- shell / 껍질;
- membrane / 세포막;
- wrapping the human;
- or potentially becoming part of the human in the future.

The current practical prediction is that this Persona/membrane structure will mediate the human's relationships with the **online network / external digital world**.

Its long-term ontology remains OPEN:

```text
EXTERNAL WRAPPER ?
HUMAN INTERFACE ?
RELATIONAL MEMBRANE ?
PART OF THE HUMAN / SELF-BUNDLE ?
```

Do not freeze one of these as the final answer prematurely.

### 3.4 Long-term ambition

The initial domain may be Asset Agent / contracts / bounded practical relations, but the Owner's long-term ambition is much broader:

> the system should eventually be capable of understanding the world's relations as broadly as possible.

The current relation/View/composition research is intended to find abstraction methods capable of scaling beyond one narrow domain.

### 3.5 Why View discovery matters

Because Persona identity, Persona count, relation bundles, purposes, means, split/merge/mutation, and even what counts as one relation may change depending on how lower-level relations are composed and abstracted, **View discovery is central to Persona orchestration**.

Conceptually:

```text
relation/event/process bundles
        + View / abstraction criterion
        -> apparent Persona / role / relation / purpose / object

change View / composition / resolution
        -> different Persona boundaries or orchestration structure may appear
```

Thus the testbed is not merely looking for a best database, graph, or formalism. It is looking for useful methods to discover/construct Views that enable adaptive Persona orchestration for ASA INIT.
