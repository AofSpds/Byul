## High-fidelity interpretation

The Owner tentatively confirms that, under the current major hypothesis, a relation-bundle we operationally call a `VIEW` is plausibly one with a strongly asymmetric dominant flow / perspective-forming behavior.

Current intuition:

- VIEW is usually not a single minimal relation but a composed relation-bundle;
- the bundle may receive / sample / route / compose a large set of world relations;
- it may produce one or more abstractions / representations / returned relation-bundles;
- smaller feedback or side-effect relations may exist in other directions;
- nevertheless, the overall relation-bundle is expected to have a dominant perspective-forming direction rather than balanced reciprocity;
- this asymmetry may be one important discriminator of VIEW-ness, but it is not yet established as a sufficient condition.

## Important open distinction

Strong asymmetry alone may not be enough to make a relation-bundle a VIEW.

Many strongly directional relation-bundles are not perspectives, for example:

- a force chain;
- a payment transfer;
- a destructive physical impact;
- a one-way command or broadcast.

A stronger candidate discriminator is that a VIEW not only has dominant directionality but also produces a relation-bundle that stands as an **informational / representational / abstraction relation** to its source domain.

This is a research candidate only.

## Research consequence

A sharper next question is:

> What extra property, beyond strong asymmetry, makes a directional relation-bundle a perspective rather than merely a one-way causal/process bundle?

Candidate distinction to test:

```text
STRONG ASYMMETRY
    +
SOURCE-REPRESENTING / ABSTRACTION-PRESERVING OUTPUT
    -> candidate VIEW-ness
```

No canonical information-theoretic, representational, semantic, or causal criterion is fixed by this note.
