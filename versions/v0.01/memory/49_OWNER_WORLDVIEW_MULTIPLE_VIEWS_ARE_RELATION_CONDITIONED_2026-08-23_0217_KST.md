## High-fidelity interpretation

- A single lower-level relation/event bundle may support multiple simultaneous higher-level views.
- There is no requirement that one bundle have one uniquely privileged abstraction.
- Which view is formed is conditioned by the relevant **relation** that the Owner had temporarily called a `protocol` in the preceding discussion.
- Therefore, view formation is not merely an external UI/query choice; under the current worldview hypothesis it may depend on a relation/context at the same general conceptual layer being discussed.

Example:

```text
E1..E5 = lower-level relation/event bundle

relation/context R1 -> View V1
relation/context R2 -> View V2
relation/context R3 -> View V3
```

Several of V1/V2/V3 may be valid at the same time for different relations/contexts/requirements.

## Terminology guard

The prior word `protocol` must not be read as a software protocol engine, registry, policy object, or implementation rule. In the current Owner worldview discussion the closer referent is a **relation / relation-level conditioning structure**.

Prefer `relation-conditioned view` in later handoff unless the Owner introduces a better term.

## Important guards

Do not infer:

`MULTIPLE_VALID_VIEWS -> ALL_VIEWS_ARE_EQUALLY_GOOD`

Do not infer:

`RELATION_CONDITIONED -> FIXED_RELATION_SCHEMA`

Do not infer:

`VIEW -> TERMINAL_OUTPUT_ONLY`

The exact relation type, view-construction formalism, and whether a view can itself participate as input/evidence in a higher-level relation bundle remain open questions.

## Research implication

This strengthens the current hypothesis that abstraction is both:

- multi-view;
- relation/context/requirement-conditioned.

A useful next unresolved interview question is whether a generated higher-level view can itself become an input/raw datum in another higher-level relation bundle, creating recursive/layered abstraction rather than a one-way terminal projection pipeline.

No canonical view algebra, relation schema, routing rule, or implementation requirement is fixed by this note.
