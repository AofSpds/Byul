## High-fidelity interpretation

The Owner distinguishes the underlying relation-first hypothesis from the convenience abstraction `relation bundle`.

Current clarification:

- a relation bundle is, for practical purposes, close to an object-like abstraction: a grouping of relations that we temporarily treat as one unit;
- therefore simply replacing the word `object` with `relation bundle` does not solve the deeper modeling problem;
- the Owner does not want the world explained primarily as a universe of fixed relation-bundle objects;
- relation bundles remain useful as derived / View-conditioned abstractions, including useful constructs such as `person`, `Persona`, `organization`, `contract`, etc.;
- a large relation bundle that we call `person` may give rise to a purpose/need relation;
- under such a purpose, a new VIEW relation or VIEW relation-bundle may be selected, composed, or even formed;
- thus Purpose may do more than merely select a pre-existing View: it may participate in the emergence/construction of a new View configuration.

Conceptually:

```text
underlying relation network
    -> some View / abstraction
    -> large relation bundle H ("person")

H + purpose/need P
    -> may select existing View V
    OR
    -> may compose/form a new View relation-bundle V'
```

## Important correction to prior framing

Do not collapse the model into:

`EVERYTHING = RELATION BUNDLE OBJECTS`.

That would merely reintroduce an object-first worldview using different vocabulary.

A safer current distinction is:

```text
RELATION = CURRENT LOWER-LEVEL MODELING CANDIDATE
RELATION BUNDLE = USEFUL DERIVED / VIEW-CONDITIONED UNIT
OBJECT = ONE POSSIBLE VIEW OF A RELATION BUNDLE
```

This remains a current hypothesis, not a final ontology.

## Purpose / View implication

Prior memory 90 established that Purpose need not be an intrinsic property inside a View and may select/use a View.

This note extends that direction:

```text
PURPOSE MAY SELECT VIEW = POSSIBLE
PURPOSE MAY CONDITION / COMPOSE / FORM VIEW = ALSO POSSIBLE
```

The exact mechanism remains OPEN.

## Research consequence

A central ASA/BYUL research question becomes:

> How can changing relations and purposes dynamically compose or form useful Views without requiring a fixed catalog of object-like View bundles in advance?

This connects directly to ASA INIT's experience-driven evolution hypothesis: accumulated experience may change the person/Persona relation structure, which may alter purpose/need relations and thereby alter or generate the Views used to interpret and act on the world.

No canonical View-construction algorithm, bundle boundary rule, purpose representation, or automatic authorization mechanism is fixed by this note.
