## High-fidelity interpretation

The Owner confirms the foundational framing that **relation is the basic modeling/worldview unit** in the current major hypothesis.

This resolves the immediately preceding question about whether an observation can be fully `read-only` in the worldview sense.

Current interpretation:

- if an observer/view and something observed are meaningfully connected, that very connection is already a relation;
- therefore observation is not modeled as the total absence of relation;
- this does **not** imply that observation must physically disturb or materially mutate the observed world;
- a practical implementation may still expose a read-only API/query/view, but at the worldview level the observational connection itself is relational;
- `read-only` is therefore an implementation/effect classification, not `no relation exists`.

Conceptually:

```text
observer / VIEW bundle
      <---- relation ---->
observed relation network

implementation effect may be:
- no external mutation
- small effect
- large effect
- derived output only

but the interaction/observation itself remains relational in the worldview.
```

## Important guard

Do not infer:

`OBSERVATION IS RELATIONAL -> OBSERVATION ALWAYS CHANGES THE PHYSICAL TARGET`.

Do not infer:

`RELATION IS BASIC UNIT -> IMPLEMENTATION MUST STORE EVERYTHING AS ONE PURE RELATION GRAPH`.

The Owner has already separated foundational relation-worldview from flexible implementation abstraction.

## Research consequence

The prior binary `relation created vs read-only View` should be reframed as:

> What kind of relation/effect does a View establish or participate in, and what consequences of that relation are represented at a chosen abstraction/resolution?

This keeps worldview semantics and implementation-side mutability separate.
