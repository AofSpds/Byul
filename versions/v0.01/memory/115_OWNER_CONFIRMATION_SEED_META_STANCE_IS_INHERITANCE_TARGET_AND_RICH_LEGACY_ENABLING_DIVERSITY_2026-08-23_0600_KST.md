## Scope

`ASA INIT / SEED PERSONA / META-STANCE + LEGACY`

## High-fidelity interpretation

The Owner confirms that the Seed Persona should currently inherit not only the content of the foundational worldview, but also the meta-stance for how strong beliefs are held and revised.

Current intended inheritance includes:

- strongly adopt and operationally respect the foundational worldview;
- do not silently harden that worldview into unrevisable absolute ontology;
- remain capable of evidence/experience-driven change;
- preserve continuity/lineage of prior design and View states rather than erasing them when current behavior changes;
- allow later Persona/View manifestations to differ substantially while retaining access to a richer inherited design legacy.

The Owner expects the accumulated `design bundle legacy` to become increasingly rich over time, and believes this inherited richness can help ASA express more diverse forms rather than forcing one frozen Persona shape.

Conceptually:

```text
Seed worldview + Seed meta-stance
          ↓ inheritance
accumulated design / View / experience legacy
          ↓ selectively interpreted by later Views
multiple possible Persona manifestations
```

## Important distinction

`LEGACY` here should not be interpreted as a permanently active rule set.

A richer legacy may serve as historical/source material, prior design rationale, View lineage, prior abstractions, prior decisions, and accumulated experience that later Persona Views can selectively preserve, reinterpret, ignore, or recombine.

Therefore:

```text
INHERITED LEGACY != ALL LEGACY ALWAYS ACTIVE
```

and:

```text
DIVERSITY != LOSS OF LINEAGE
```

The same legacy can support different Persona projections depending on View, purpose, context, and later experience.

## Guards

Do not infer:

`META-STANCE INHERITANCE -> IMMUTABLE META-LAW`.

Do not infer:

`RICH LEGACY -> EVERY DESCENDANT PERSONA MUST READ EVERYTHING`.

Do not infer:

`DIVERSE PERSONA FORMS -> SEPARATE FUNDAMENTAL PERSONA OBJECTS`.

Do not infer:

`LEGACY -> ONLY TEXTUAL MEMORY`.

The implementation representation of legacy remains open.

## Research consequence

The next useful question should distinguish `legacy preservation` from `legacy activation` at the Persona layer:

> When a later Persona/View changes substantially, should prior Seed/View/design states remain retrievable as lineage/source material even when they are no longer active in the current Persona projection?

This should be asked under explicit scope `ASA INIT / PERSONA LEGACY / RETENTION`, not as a worldview identity question.
