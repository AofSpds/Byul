## Terminology note

For Owner-facing discussion, prefer Korean terminology first and add English only in parentheses when useful.

`derived relation` should be explained as `도출된 관계` or `파생 관계`: a relation/result obtained by applying a View or composition, without implying that the underlying world-relation network has been ontologically rewritten.

## High-fidelity interpretation

The Owner clarifies three linked points.

### 1. VIEW has its own lifecycle

A VIEW relation-bundle is not assumed eternal or immutable.
It may form, be used, change, persist for some scope, lose usefulness, be replaced, or disappear under its own View-conditioned lifecycle.

### 2. A relation composed inside a VIEW does not automatically rewrite the underlying world

If a View treats:

```text
A -> B -> C
```

as a higher-level relation:

```text
A -> C
```

that composed relation is meaningful **inside that View / abstraction**.
It should not be interpreted as evidence that the underlying world was physically or ontologically changed merely because the View composed it that way.

This is better described as a `도출 관계 / 파생 관계` rather than as a new foundational world relation by default.

### 3. VIEW is expected to be strongly asymmetric / predominantly one-way

Because the current basic relation hypothesis is directional and transformational, a practical VIEW relation-bundle is expected to have a strong dominant direction.

Conceptually:

```text
world/source relation network
        >>> dominant observation / routing / abstraction direction >>>
VIEW relation-bundle
        >>> large derived representation / returned source-target bundle >>>

while smaller feedback/influence relations may also exist in other directions
```

The Owner does not claim perfect isolation: VIEW and observed world may influence each other through other relations. But what makes a VIEW useful as a perspective may be that its relation-bundle has a highly asymmetric dominant flow rather than a balanced bidirectional relation.

## Important guards

Do not infer:

`VIEW IS ONE-WAY -> NO FEEDBACK OR INFLUENCE EXISTS`.

Do not infer:

`VIEW OUTPUT -> UNDERLYING WORLD IS REWRITTEN`.

Do not infer:

`DERIVED RELATION -> FALSE OR UNREAL`.

A derived/composed relation can be operationally meaningful within the View even if it is not a new foundational relation in the underlying world model.

Do not infer:

`VIEW LIFECYCLE -> ONE UNIVERSAL LIFECYCLE MODEL`.

Lifecycle remains View-conditioned and implementation representation remains open.

## Research consequence

A strong next research axis is to characterize VIEW by **asymmetric flow / dominant directionality** rather than merely by stored properties:

- what is the dominant source side?
- what kind of target/representation does the View produce?
- what smaller feedback or side-effect relations are allowed?
- how does the View lifecycle start/change/end?
- when does a derived relation remain local to the View versus become materialized/admitted into another relation network?

No canonical port model, flow metric, effect threshold, lifecycle schema, or materialization rule is fixed by this note.
