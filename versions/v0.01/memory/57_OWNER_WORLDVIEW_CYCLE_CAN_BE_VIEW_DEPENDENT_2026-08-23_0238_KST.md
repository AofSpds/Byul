## Owner statement

> "네 맞습니다. 그런 view도 있을수 있습니다. View에 따라 다릅니ㅣㄷㆍ"

## Referent

The immediately preceding example distinguished:

```text
high resolution:
A_t0 -> B_t1 -> A_t2

coarser view:
A -> B -> A
```

and asked whether a cycle-like pattern may arise as a lower-resolution/view-relative representation even when the higher-resolution chain is future-directed.

## High-fidelity interpretation

- Yes: a cycle-like representation may be a valid view of a finer future-directed relation chain.
- Whether the structure appears cyclic can therefore depend on the active view / abstraction / resolution.
- This is consistent with the Owner's broader worldview that identity, atomicity, resolution, worldview, and abstraction are relation/view-conditioned rather than globally fixed.
- The answer does **not** exclude the possibility that some other view or formalism may represent genuinely recurrent structures; it only confirms that apparent cycles need not be ontologically primitive or resolution-invariant.

## Important guards

Do not infer:

`CYCLE_IS_ALWAYS_DERIVED_VIEW`

or

`HIGHEST_RESOLUTION_WORLDVIEW_IS_PROVEN_DAG`

or

`ALL_RELATIONS_ARE_STRICTLY_LINEAR`.

The current confirmed point is narrower:

`CYCLE_APPEARANCE_CAN_BE_VIEW_DEPENDENT`.

## Interview implication

Avoid repeatedly asking whether cycles are "real" versus "view" in absolute terms. Future questions should move to genuinely unresolved modeling issues or to how these worldview hypotheses can be operationalized/tested without prematurely fixing an implementation.

No graph topology, causality formalism, cycle semantics, or implementation requirement is fixed by this note.
