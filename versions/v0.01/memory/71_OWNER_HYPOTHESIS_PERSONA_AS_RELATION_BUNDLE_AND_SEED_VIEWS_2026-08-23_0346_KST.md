## High-fidelity interpretation

The Owner does **not** propose one canonical Persona-splitting rule.

The current working direction is instead:

1. ASSET AI AGENT ASA INIT should likely ship with **several initial criteria / Views** as bootstrap lenses.
2. These Views are not final truth or universal Persona boundaries; they are initial abstraction criteria for interpreting relation bundles.
3. Under the Owner's current hypothesis, a Persona itself may be modeled not as a fixed primitive object, but as a **bundle of relations** recognized/constructed under a View.
4. Therefore Persona count, boundaries, split/merge, and apparent individuality may depend on the active View and relation composition.
5. The practical research problem becomes: which seed Views are useful enough for INIT, how they produce different Persona decompositions, and how the system can later discover/revise/add Views through use and empirical feedback.

Conceptually:

```text
relation network R
   + View V1 -> Persona bundle P1
   + View V2 -> Persona bundles P2, P3
   + View V3 -> one broader Persona P0
```

The relation network need not physically change for apparent Persona structure to change; the abstraction criterion may change.

## Research consequence

ASA INIT Persona Orchestration should investigate a **small bootstrap View set** rather than a single fixed Persona classifier.

Candidate View families are not yet Owner-approved. Future research may compare, for example, purpose/goal-oriented, relation-counterparty, memory/history, authority/responsibility, task/process, temporal/lifecycle, or other prior-art-grounded abstraction criteria. These are research candidates only until empirically studied.

The key target is not to decide a universal number of Personas, but to provide useful abstraction criteria by which relation bundles can appear as Persona structures appropriate to the active purpose and context.
