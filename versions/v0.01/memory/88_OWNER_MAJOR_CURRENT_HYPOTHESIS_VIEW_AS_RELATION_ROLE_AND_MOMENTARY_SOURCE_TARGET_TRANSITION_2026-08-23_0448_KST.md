## Owner technical statement

> "관계가 어떤 가진 속성에 의해 우리가 VIEW라고 구분하는 관계가 있다. 그리고 VIEW의 관계속성은 세상을 보기 위한 관점이 될수 있는 속성을 갖고 있다."

The Owner further clarified the source/target intuition:

- a source passes through a relation/transformation and becomes a target;
- that target may immediately become the next source;
- source and target are therefore momentary roles/states within an ongoing relation network, not permanently fixed ontological categories;
- what is observed as a `state of the world` may be only a momentary projection of a continuously changing relation network.

## High-fidelity interpretation

Current major hypothesis:

1. `VIEW` is not assumed to be a separate primitive/meta-object outside the relation model.
2. Some relations / relation bundles have properties or capabilities by which they can function as a perspective over other relations; those are operationally distinguished as `VIEW`.
3. A View may therefore participate in transformation/routing rather than merely label a static snapshot.
4. `SOURCE` and `TARGET` are role-like and transient:

```text
S_t0 --relation / mapping / transformation--> T_t1
T_t1 may then play SOURCE role in the next relation
T_t1 --relation / mapping / transformation--> T_t2
```

5. A world-state representation is therefore better treated as a temporary View-conditioned state of an evolving relation network rather than as a permanently fixed object-state.

## Important distinction

Do not infer:

`SOURCE != TARGET AS PERMANENT TYPES`.

Current hypothesis is closer to:

`SOURCE_ROLE / TARGET_ROLE = CONTEXTUAL, TEMPORARY RELATION ROLES`.

Do not infer:

`MOMENTARY STATE = COMPLETE REALITY`.

The represented state remains an abstraction/View over reality.

Do not infer:

`VIEW PARTICIPATES IN TRANSFORMATION -> EVERY VIEW MUST MUTATE THE UNDERLYING WORLD`.

A View may produce a new representation, derived relation bundle, routing result, or other target state without implying external-world mutation. The exact execution semantics remain OPEN.

## Research consequence

A central next question is how to distinguish:

- observational/projection View: produces a derived target representation;
- transformational View: changes or produces a successor relation-state;
- mixed/recursive View: its output becomes input/source for later Views.

No canonical View type taxonomy, operator semantics, relation algebra, temporal model, or persistence scheme is fixed by this note.
