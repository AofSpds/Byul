## Referent being confirmed

The immediately preceding interview proposed that what appears as a `purpose` at one level/view may appear as a `means`, intermediate target, or sub-route when embedded inside a broader composite relation/function.

Example intuition:

```text
A --f--> B --g--> C

under local view of f:
B = target / local purpose

under composite view g∘f:
B = intermediate / means
C = higher-level target / purpose
```

The Owner confirms that this is the intended meaning of the earlier composite-function intuition.

## High-fidelity interpretation

- `purpose`, `means`, `detour`, and `intermediate target` should not automatically be treated as intrinsic, globally fixed types of a node/relation/event;
- their role may depend on the active View, relation, resolution, and composite path being considered;
- a target at one local mapping can become an intermediate state/means inside a larger composition;
- this is a direct application of the Owner's broader current hypothesis that relations/mappings can compose recursively and that abstraction/view determines how the bundle is interpreted;
- therefore purpose-achievement may be understood through composition rather than a permanently typed hierarchy.

## Why globally fixed purpose/means types can be dangerous

A globally fixed ontology such as:

```text
NODE B.type = PURPOSE
```

can create several distortions:

1. **Composition breakage** — the same B cannot naturally serve as the codomain/goal of `f` and the intermediate domain/input of `g` inside `g∘f`.
2. **View collapse** — the model silently privileges one view/resolution by freezing the role assigned under that view.
3. **Routing rigidity** — when route A fails, the system may preserve the typed local goal even when another composite route can achieve the broader purpose more effectively.
4. **False failure/success classification** — failure of a local sub-goal may be misreported as failure of the broader purpose, or completion of a local target may be mistaken for completion of the composite purpose.
5. **Recursive abstraction conflict** — a higher-level View that should become an input to another relation bundle is obstructed if its current output label is treated as terminal.
6. **Goalpost-guard overreach** — a rigid anti-goalpost mechanism may incorrectly prohibit legitimate recomposition/route changes because it confuses changing a local means with changing the orienting purpose.

## Safer current research hypothesis

Instead of intrinsic typing:

```text
B IS PURPOSE
```

prefer a contextual role statement such as:

```text
Role(B | View V, Composite Path C, Relation R) = PURPOSE
```

while another view/path may yield:

```text
Role(B | View V2, Composite Path C2, Relation R2) = MEANS / INTERMEDIATE
```

This is a research interpretation, not a frozen implementation schema.

## Important guards

Do not infer:

`PURPOSE/MEANS_VIEW_DEPENDENT -> NO GOALS EXIST`

Do not infer:

`COMPOSITION -> ALL PURPOSES REDUCE TO ONE GLOBAL SUPER-PURPOSE`

Do not infer:

`ROLE_CONTEXTUALITY -> ANY ROUTE COUNTS AS SUCCESS`

Historical frame/result preservation still applies. A local failure remains a failure under the frame in which it was evaluated, even if a broader composite route later succeeds.
