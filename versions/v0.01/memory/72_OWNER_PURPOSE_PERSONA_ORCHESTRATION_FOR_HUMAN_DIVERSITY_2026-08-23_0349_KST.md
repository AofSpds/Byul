## High-fidelity interpretation

The Owner confirms the project purpose more precisely:

1. ASA INIT must begin with several human-designed seed Views because a system that has no initial way to see/interpret relations cannot establish meaningful relations at all.
2. These seed Views are bootstrap instruments, not a final taxonomy of human identity or Persona structure.
3. The project explicitly aims for later diversity and evolution beyond the initial Views.
4. The practical objective is Persona Orchestration capable of providing adaptable forms — metaphorically `그릇 / 옷 / 가면` — for diverse humans and diverse viewpoints.
5. Therefore the target is not one ideal Persona architecture, one ideal Persona count, or one universal View. The target is an orchestration substrate that can host, generate, adapt, or discover Persona configurations appropriate to different people, contexts, relations, and Views.

Current purpose statement:

> **인간은 다양하고 관점도 다양하다. ASSET AI AGENT ASA INIT은 그 다양한 인간과 관점이 자신에게 맞는 Persona 구성으로 세계와 관계를 맺을 수 있도록, 몇 개의 seed View에서 시작해 다양한 Persona의 형성·분화·합성·진화를 허용하는 Persona Orchestration 기반을 제공하는 것을 목표로 한다.**

## Why seed Views are unavoidable

The Owner's statement `처음에 볼 수가 없으면 (관계를 맺을 수가 없으면) 어쩌겠어요` establishes a bootstrap constraint:

```text
NO INITIAL VIEW
    -> NO PRACTICAL INTERPRETATION
    -> NO PRACTICAL RELATION FORMATION
```

Thus several Views must initially be designed by the project. This is a practical necessity, not a claim that those Views are ontologically privileged.

## Persona-as-relation-bundle connection

Under the Owner's current working hypothesis:

```text
relation network
   + seed/discovered View
   -> relation bundle
   -> apparent/operational Persona
```

If this hypothesis proves implementation-suitable, Persona orchestration becomes the problem of discovering and operating useful relation bundles under multiple evolving Views rather than managing a permanently fixed list of Persona objects.

## Diversity target

The project should preserve room for:

- different people to require different Persona configurations;
- the same person to present different Persona bundles under different Views;
- Persona bundles to split, merge, mutate, succeed, or be reinterpreted;
- new Views to be discovered or constructed after INIT;
- initial seed Views to be revised or retired when empirical use shows better alternatives.

This diversity target must not be overread as a requirement that all possible Views or Personas coexist simultaneously.

## Guards

Do not infer:

`SEED_VIEWS -> FINAL_VIEW_SET`

Do not infer:

`HUMAN_DIVERSITY -> EVERY PERSON MUST HAVE MANY PERSONAS`

Do not infer:

`PERSONA_ORCHESTRATION -> PERSONA_IS ONLY A MASK`

The metaphors `그릇 / 옷 / 가면` express adaptability of form, not a final ontology. The earlier shell/membrane/wrapper/part-of-human question remains OPEN.

Do not infer:

`DIVERSE_EVOLUTION -> UNBOUNDED RANDOM MUTATION`

Evolution remains purpose-, relation-, View-, evidence-, and governance-conditioned research territory.

## Research consequence

The immediate BYUL testbed question is no longer simply `what is a Persona?`

It is closer to:

> **What seed Views and abstraction/composition methods allow ASA INIT to bootstrap useful Persona relation-bundles, while preserving the ability for different humans, contexts, and future evidence to produce different Persona organizations?**

No specific seed View set, discovery algorithm, split/merge rule, or orchestration implementation is fixed by this note.
