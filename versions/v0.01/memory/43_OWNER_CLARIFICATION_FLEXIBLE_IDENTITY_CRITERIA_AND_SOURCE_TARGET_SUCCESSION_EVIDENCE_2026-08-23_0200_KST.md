> "같다는 건 특정 기준에 충족하는 것이라 주관적인것이고 우린 이 기준도 유연하게 볼겁니다.

## High-fidelity interpretation

- `same / identity` is not assumed to be an absolute primitive fact.
- Whether two states/models/entities count as "the same" depends on a chosen criterion or view.
- BYUL should allow that criterion itself to be flexible and context-sensitive rather than globally frozen.
- A baseline piece of evidence for **succession / continuity** is whether a source and target are connected by an explicit transition/mapping/lineage relation.
- `source -> target connected` is therefore treated as **evidence of succession**, not automatically as proof of semantic identity under every criterion.

## Important guard

Do not collapse:

`SOURCE_TARGET_CONNECTION`
into
`ABSOLUTE_IDENTITY`

or

`SUCCESSION_EVIDENCE`
into
`SUCCESSION_SUFFICIENCY_FOR_ALL_VIEWS`.

The later criterion for calling two states/models/entities "the same" may vary by purpose, preservation demand, scale, context, or model/view.

## Interview implication

Future questions should not ask the Owner to choose one fixed global identity rule. Instead, investigate:

- what kinds of criteria may be used in different contexts;
- what evidence is needed to support succession under those criteria;
- when a source→target connection is sufficient, insufficient, or ambiguous;
- how conflicting identity/succession views should coexist or be resolved.
